While at Cisco's annual kickoff (known as Cisco GSX), I saw a presentation by John Parello about the "Internet of Agents" and the AGNTCY project that enables AI agents from different organizations to discover, connect, and collaborate seamlessly across boundaries. To illustrate this in action, Aditi Gupta gave us an overview of coffeeAgntcy - a reference implementation based on a fictitious coffee company that shows how the components of AGNTCY work together as a Multi-agent System.
