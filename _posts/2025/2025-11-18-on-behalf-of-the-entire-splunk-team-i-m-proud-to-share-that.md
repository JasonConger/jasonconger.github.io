On behalf of the entire Splunk team, I'm proud to share that Splunk has been named a 2025 Microsoft Americas Partner of the Year Finalist in the Software Development Company (SDC) award category. It has been great to see the progression from foundational integrations with Azure, Microsoft 365, Defender, Entra, etc. in Splunk, to brining Splunk Cloud fully hosted in Azure and transactional via the Azure Marketplace, to becoming a Partner of the Year Finalist.  Thank you Microsoft for the continued partnership!
