Splunk provides many methods to gain insight into data, and we strive to meet customers where they are. Now, users of Microsoft Security Copilot can interact with Splunk via a natural language AI interface. I recently had a chance to talk with Wayne Brown about the Splunk plugin and get a demo of how it works. Security Copilot takes natural language, transforms it into Splunk Search Processing Language (SPL), dispatches searches, and displays results while keeping session context.
