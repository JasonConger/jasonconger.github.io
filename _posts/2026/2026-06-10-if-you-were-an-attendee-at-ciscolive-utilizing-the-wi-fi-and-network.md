If you were an attendee at #CiscoLive utilizing the Wi-Fi and network infrastructure, we had your back for cybersecurity by hosting a SOC right inside the World of Solutions show floor. While at the conference, I had a chance to chat with Dan Christiansen, CISSP about what it takes to have a SOC at and industry event, how the SOC operates, and all the technology used.  Using Splunk, we

➡️ Leveraged Splunk Enterprise Security, Attack Analyzer, and Triage Agent to automate level one detection and investigation
➡️ Combined network telemetry and firewall data 
➡️ Utilized XDR with the Agentic SOC
➡️  Detected clear text password and unusual network activity

There's more than meets the eye to secure an event like this, and the crew takes this SOC on the road to other conferences and event - including the upcoming 2026 Olympics. This was an eye-opening conversation to me to get a glimpse into what it takes to run a SOC for an event.
