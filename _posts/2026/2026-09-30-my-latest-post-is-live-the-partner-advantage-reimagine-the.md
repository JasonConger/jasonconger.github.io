My latest post is live: "The Partner Advantage: Reimagine the Foundation for the Agentic Era."

Splunk .conf26 introduced "Splunk Reimagined" which is the idea that the constraints that have shaped enterprise data architecture for twenty years (spend, skill, speed) can finally be lifted together, not chipped away at one at a time. For partners, every constraint removed is a service line.

In this one I dig into:
➡️ Cisco AI POD for Splunk - bringing AI capabilities to on-prem and air-gapped environments
➡️ Cisco Data Fabric - right data, right place, right analytics for human and AI scale
➡️ The Agentic SOC and Agent Observability - defending at machine speed while keeping humans in or on the loop

The thread running through all of it is that the partners getting real results aren't the ones deploying the most agents the fastest. Instead, they're the ones insisting on getting the foundation right in order to make agentic action trustworthy in the first place.
