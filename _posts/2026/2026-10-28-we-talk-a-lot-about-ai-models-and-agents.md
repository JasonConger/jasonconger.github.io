We talk a lot about AI models and agents. But there's a whole data center running them, and it needs security too.

At Cisco Partner Summit I talked with Jerry Lin, Global Principal Security Architect at Cisco, about how to layer security into the AI-ready data center. He broke it into three parts:

 1️⃣ Protect east-west traffic and workloads. Modernize the firewall (including Cisco's Encrypted Visibility Engine, which sees into encrypted traffic without decrypting it), then push segmentation into the network itself: Nexus smart switches enforce L4 policy at the switch, all managed from Cisco Cloud Control. As Jerry put it, segmentation "is not about installing a bunch of firewalls. It doesn't scale."
 2️⃣ Secure the AI apps. AI Defense gives visibility into AI traffic, validates the models customers download, and helps build custom guardrails, because "one guardrail can't protect both of us equally."
 3️⃣ Make the infrastructure itself resilient, with features like Live Protect on Nexus switches.
