Patching network infrastructure can take customers weeks, months, sometimes an entire year. What if you could mitigate the threat in minutes, with no reboot?

At Cisco Partner Summit I talked with Tyson Scott, Technical Solutions Architect at Cisco, about Cisco Live Protect. (This picks up where my conversation with Jerry Lin on securing the AI-ready data center left off.)

How it works:

🔹 Live Protect delivers zero-day vulnerability protection by deploying "shields" through an agent running on the switches and routers themselves.
🔹 When Cisco announces a PSIRT advisory, customers can deploy protection the same day.
🔹 A shield is a small YAML configuration applied to the switch, with "no reboot, no software update, no patching."
🔹 It's available today in NX-OS and Catalyst Center, with vManage and other controller-based platforms coming.

Tyson's point: there's no other OEM or competitor with a similar offering.
