Your AI app is live. Now how do you monitor it? AI applications don't behave like the apps traditional observability was built for.

At our Data Strategy Workshop in Singapore, I caught up again with Savitalaxmi Vishwanathan, Global Partner Technical Manager at Splunk, to talk about what agent observability does once AI is in production:

 ➡️ Manage your AI agents and their behaviors
 ➡️ See end-to-end user interactions
 ➡️ Track tokenomics, "because that's the talk of the town"
 ➡️ Guardrails, with policies on top of your agents

How does it evaluate all of that? There are 30+ evaluators out of the box, plus support for large language models when every organization wants to check its apps its own way. The differentiator is Luna, a small language model. Start on the LLM, then move to the SLM and save on cost and resources.

Next up after the Cisco Data Fabric workshops is Agent Observability workshops for partners.
