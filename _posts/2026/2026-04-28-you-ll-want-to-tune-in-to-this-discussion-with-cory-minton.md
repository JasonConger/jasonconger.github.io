You'll want to tune in to this discussion with Cory Minton and Peter Bailey!  I always gain fresh insights when hearing both Cory and Peter present their thoughts on trends in the broader technology context.  And, this one is all about security speed, efficiency, and scale.

Attackers are industrializing exploitation, and defenders are increasingly relying on automated environments.  This is causing the gap between exposure and response to narrow.  This session will be about what changes when both defenders and adversaries operate at machine speed.
