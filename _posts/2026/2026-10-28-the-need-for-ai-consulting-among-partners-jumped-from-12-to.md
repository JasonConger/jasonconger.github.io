The need for AI consulting among partners jumped from 12% to 50%.

That was the number one change in this year's Partner AI Readiness Survey, according to Robin Aguirre, Strategy and Sales Enablement Leader for Global Partner Engineering at Cisco. We caught up at Cisco Partner Summit to talk about what the survey found.

A few things that stood out:
 ➡️ It's the third year of the survey (2024, 2025 and 2026), run double-blind by a third-party agency and aimed at decision makers across every geo.
 ➡️ This year the focus moved "from adoption to accountability," with a real emphasis on ROI.
 ➡️ Every year the survey shows bigger expectations for how much AI will impact customers' businesses.

Check out full results here - <https://lnkd.in/gMDzKF27>

For partners, that's a clear signal of where to focus your practice and where the market is heading. Robin says the results and a white paper will be on the Cisco partner page after Partner Summit.
