Another Splunk .conf in the books! 🎤 

"Splunk Reimagined" was a prevalent theme, and big part of that story is data strategy. With Cisco Data Fabric powered by the Splunk Platform, the pitch isn't "move all your data into one place" anymore. Instead, it's all about making data usable wherever it is. Federated search, a Machine Data Lake that keeps cost sane at massive scale, and AI experiences to get the right data to the right place to drive the right analytics are just the start. That shift alone changes how organizations plan their next five years of infrastructure.

It was also great getting time with partners this week. They're in the trenches turning these announcements into real customer outcomes. That's always the best part of .conf for me.

Add in a customer cutting incident response time from 20 minutes to 39 seconds, a coffee maker triggered by an AI agent on the keynote stage (yes, really), and a closing night set from Ludacris that had the whole crowd moving, and you've got a good week.

Every year this community reminds me why I love doing this. See you in Chicago for .conf27.
