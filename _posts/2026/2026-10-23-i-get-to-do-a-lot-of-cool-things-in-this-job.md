I get to do a lot of cool things in this job. Spending two days in Singapore with 50+ partner engineers and practice leads is near the top of the list.

That time is the most valuable thing a partner can give, and we don't take it for granted. They didn't just sit and listen, either. They broke into rooms to work through real customer scenarios, then spent a full day building in the hands-on labs with Cisco Data Fabric powered by the Splunk Platform.

Between sessions I grabbed a few people for quick interviews on data strategy, AI safety, monitoring AI agents, and how telcos are reinventing themselves. I had a the opportunity to chat with many partners about what they're seeing in the field and the challenges their customers are facing. It's an exciting time to be in technology!

A few thank-you's:
➡️ Every partner who spent your time with us, for your questions and your energy!

➡️ Amit Harjani for leading the program, and a huge shout out to our execs Alex Pujols, PhD, Aly Shivji, Akther Ahmed, Robert Pizzari, and Adrian Pok for leaning in.
