Here we go - time for Splunk .conf26! I've been talking about developing on top of Splunk for several years, and this year I get to present along 2 great Splunkers about Visual Studio Code, using AI in development, debugging, and making beautiful use cases for your data. 

 ➡️ Jason Green will walk us through his contributions to the Splunk Visual Studio Code extension to make lives easer for admins. 
 ➡️ I'll be talking about debugging and using AI in your dev cycle.
 ➡️ Simon Ric - Hansen will share how to make stunning use cases out of the data you have access to (with some AI magic in there).

If you are going to be onsite, join us for DEV1419 <https://lnkd.in/gvdtNPBG>

Happy Splunking!
