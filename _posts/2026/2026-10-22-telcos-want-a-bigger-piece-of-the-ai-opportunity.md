Telcos want a bigger piece of the AI opportunity. But selling networking, firewalls and switches is a very different business from selling AI outcomes.

At our Data Strategy Workshop in Singapore I caught up with Albert Kurnia, Partner Account Executive at Cisco, about how telco partners can reinvent themselves.

A few things that stood out:

 ➡️ Start with the why. Why does the customer want AI, and why do they want to control their data? That takes a real understanding of data.
 ➡️ Change who you talk to. Get in front of the CIOs, the CISOs and even the CEOs, and learn their most pressing business problems.
 ➡️ Prove the value. Help customers get real business impact, like measurable in ROI, as a joint partnership.
