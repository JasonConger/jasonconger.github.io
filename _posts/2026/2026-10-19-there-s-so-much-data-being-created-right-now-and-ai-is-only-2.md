There's so much data being created right now, and AI is only adding more. Your data strategy decides how much value you actually get out of it.

This week we ran a Data Strategy Workshop for Splunk partners for the first time in Tokyo. I grabbed Travis Kane (aka T-Rex) who built a lot of the hands-on material, to explain what the Cisco Data Fabric means for partners and their customers.

Trav breaks it down into what he calls the "T-Rex three pillars of data value realization":

1️⃣ Stream: change, redact, route and manipulate data on the fly (Ingest Actions, Ingest Processor, Edge Processor, plus AI-powered data onboarding)

2️⃣ Land: put each kind of data where it makes sense for performance and price, including Machine Data Lake for long-term retention

3️⃣ Analyze: native Splunk, AI-based, or MCP, so you can bring our own interface to analyze that data at scale from anywhere.

The best part of the day was watching partners work through real customer challenges and present back how they would apply it.
