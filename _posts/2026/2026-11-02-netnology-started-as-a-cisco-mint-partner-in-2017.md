Netnology started as a Cisco MINT partner in 2017. Ten Partner Summits later, and they've been recognized as one of Cisco's ALLY MINT Partners of the Year again.

Congratulations to Salman Asadullah, Managing Principal at Netnology. We caught up at Cisco Partner Summit in Vancouver to talk about the journey:

➡️ From one Cisco architecture to four: enterprise, data center, security and observability
➡️ Over 500 engagements delivered for Cisco
➡️ MINT Partner of the Year in 2023, a Tier 1 MINT Partner for services excellence in 2025, and an ALLY MINT Partner of the Year again in 2026

Salman talks about the recognition. "It's a huge honor, but at the same time it also brings more responsibility" to keep delivering with excellence on complex, strategic engagements. And he credits the team: mostly former Cisco engineers and architects whose depth shows up in every project.
