"You notice I said activate, not enable." Thats how Amit Harjani, Head of Global Partner Solution Engineering at Splunk, describes the Data Strategy Workshops we've been running across APJC. Tokyo first, now Singapore.

The usual model is to fly in, do two hours of enablement, and head out the door. This is different:
🔹 Day one, first half: the new data strategy innovations from .conf and how to run a data strategy workshop
🔹 Day one, second half: partners break out into rooms and work on real scenarios they have with their own customers
🔹 Day two: a hands-on workshop lab

As we filmed this, partners were spread across six or seven rooms in the Singapore office building their plans. The feedback in both cities has been the same: they've never had this kind of engagement before.

And theres more coming. Amit shares whats next in the pipeline, including a Splunk Agent Observability workshop and Agentic SOC.
