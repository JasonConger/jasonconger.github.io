\#CiscoLive Day 2 is kicking off and the conversations have been incredible so far.  Partners are innovating in the new rapidly-changing world, and we're seeing three main categories:

✅  Operational Simplicity
🎛️  Tackling one of the largest sources of down time - data sprawl.  Cisco Data Fabric allows Splunk to use data where it is, intelligently route the right data to the right place to drive the right analytics, and provides AI experiences to accelerate success.
🛠️ Tool rationalization - we have see that up to 85% of data sources already in Splunk can be used for multiple use cases.  Partners are helping customers realize more value from their tools and reducing silo friction.

✅  Infuse, observe, and protect AI systems
☁️ Cisco Cloud Control, AI Canvas, AI Assistants, etc. can all be integrated into partner solutions.
👓  Splunk provides visibility into AI systems including prompt/response observability, token utilization, infrastructure monitoring, agent behavior, model behavior, and more to minimize trust deficits.

✅  Maximize speed and efficiency 
🔐 We are moving at machine speed, and a single human user may have multiple agents working on their behalf. Identity, access, and behavior are integral to ensuring systems are resilient.  We are using AI in our products to accelerate detection, investigation, and response.  From keeping systems resilient to down time or attacks, we're giving analysts the right tools at the right time.
