2,117 assets affected by 146 critical or high advisories. The first question everybody asks is "great, now what?"

At Cisco Partner Summit I caught up with Jason Coleman, Principal Architect at Cisco, for a live demo of two things in Cisco IQ that answer exactly that question:

1️⃣ Estate composition: see your support tiers, asset importance, LDOS and coverage status today, then slide the timeline forward up to two years to see what falls out of coverage before it happens.

2️⃣ Resilient Infrastructure Service (RIS): map your attack surface from telemetry, rank assets by how critical they are to the business, and know which ones to fix first. In the demo, 349 of 2,100 affected assets were critical, while 47 low-importance ones are the perfect place to test the upgrade first.

Getting started takes about five minutes at iq.cisco.com.
