Two days away from customers is a big ask for a partner. In Tokyo, 50+ of them said yes.

Partner engineers, sellers and practice leads joined us for the Splunk Partner Data Strategy Workshop, digging into data strategy and Cisco Data Fabric powered by the Splunk Platform. When partners invest their time like that, it’s greatly appreciated, and it doesn’t go unnoticed.

I had the pleasure of kicking off Day 1 with the "why". A machine data strategy is the foundation, and the real hero. Machine data is a lot like dashcam footage. When things are going well, you don't think about it. Then something happens, and every second matters. It had better be there. So three questions: What do we record? Where do we keep it? How do we use it?

Then the partners took over. They brought real customer scenarios, spent a full day building in the labs, and kept tying the technology back to services their customers will need.

Customers are increasingly leaning on partners to assess their data, act on a plan, and keep optimizing it. The people in this room are ready for that conversation.

Listen to your data. Just not all of it the same way.
