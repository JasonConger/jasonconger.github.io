Convergent and Divergent Thinking in the Age of AI - that is a topic I had the chance to explore with Annie Hardy while at Cisco Live recently. In a nutshell, convergent thinking focuses on finding a logical and best solution to a problem. It is factual, logical, and systematic. Divergent thinking focuses on generating creative possibilities and ideas via brainstorming. It is free-flowing, non-linear, and outside the box. AI is really good at convergence. Strategists are really good at divergence. It isn't ultra easy to be good at both forms of thinking and, as Annie states, "...leaders have to figure out how to bridge that gap."

The book Annie mentions is "Paddle Forward: Teaming in the Age of AI" by Pat Bodin ( <https://lnkd.in/gR4A83-C> )
